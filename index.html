01 / The vision

Why run AI
locally?

You should be able to use capable AI without sending every request to a remote server.

Cloud AI is useful. But for private work, unreliable connections, or simply having control over your tools, there’s a reason to run models locally.

That’s the problem we’re working on: dedicated inference hardware, with the software needed to use it on personal computers, developer workstations, and eventually embedded systems.

I

Keep work local.

Our goal is to process sensitive workloads on your own hardware, alongside your data.

II

Use AI offline.

Local inference should work without a continuous connection to a data center.

III

Choose where it runs.

Make locally owned hardware a practical option for everyday AI use.

02 / The research

Building AI Key.

Unlock local inference.

Reprogrammable hardware.
Purpose-built for AI inference.

AI Key is our USB-C accelerator concept for running capable AI models locally. It brings together reprogrammable inference hardware and the software to use it. Our current work focuses on the architecture, model validation, and hardware design needed to move from simulations to a physical prototype.

Our ambition reaches beyond the accelerator: a complete local AI system, from the hardware to the software and setup experience.

HARDWARESOFTWARESETUP

The full system

Hardware, software,
and setup.

We’re developing the accelerator, supporting software, and setup as one system. Local control and protection for model owners’ intellectual property are design requirements we’re working toward.

We intend to support licensed proprietary models running locally without exposing their weights. This goal informs both the hardware and software research from the beginning.

The development plan.
